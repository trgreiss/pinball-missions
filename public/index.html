How it works

  • Pick the machines at your location on the Machines page (or import them from Pinball Map).
  • Generate a mission: one questline per machine. A questline is an ordered chain of goals from that machine's rulesheet, e.g. qualify a multiball → start it → collect jackpots → score the super jackpot → reach the mini-wizard mode.
  • Read the whole questline before you plunge. You can't pause mid-game, so keep in mind what comes after each step.
  • Check off each step you reach. Your next mission picks up where you left off. Switch to any other questline for a machine with its dropdown.
  • Follow the rulesheet link on any step for the full details.
